Soundrise
---
**Your morning playlist. Ready when you are.**

Soundrise is an alarm for iPhone that has your music ready
before you're awake enough to choose it.

Set your wake time, pick genres or a playlist from your Apple
Music library, and Soundrise builds you a fresh set for the
length of morning you actually have. When the alarm sounds,
your soundtrack is waiting.

---

## How it works

**Choose your music.** Pick genres in the order you want them,
or point Soundrise at a playlist you already love.

**Set the length.** 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Soundrise builds
a set to fit.

**Wake up.** The alarm sounds. Your playlist is ready to
start.

---

## Requires Apple Music

Soundrise plays from your own Apple Music library, so an
active Apple Music subscription is needed for full playback.
