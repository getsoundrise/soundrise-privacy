## Common questions

**Do I need Apple Music?**
Yes. Soundrise plays from your Apple Music library, so an
active subscription is needed for full playback.

**Why doesn't the music start on its own when the alarm goes
off?**
Soundrise plays a reliable alarm tone, and you tap to start
your playlist. This is deliberate — it means the alarm always
works, even if music can't load for any reason.

**How do I change my music?**
Tap "Change music" on the home screen, or go to Settings and
run setup again.

**How long is my playlist?**
As long as you set it — 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. If the
playlist or genres you chose don't have enough songs to fill
that time, Soundrise uses what's there.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Delete My Data removes your saved preferences and
cancels any scheduled alarms. Deleting the app does the same.

**How do I stop Soundrise accessing my music?**
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.
There's a shortcut to this in Soundrise's own Settings screen.
