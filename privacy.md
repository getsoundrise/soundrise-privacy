Privacy Policy
---
**Last updated: 8 September 2026**

## Summary

Soundrise does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
information. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

## What Soundrise accesses

**Your Apple Music library.** With your permission, Soundrise
reads your music library — your playlists, songs, artists,
albums, and genres — so it can build a morning playlist for
you. This information is read on your device, is used only to
create and play your playlist, and is never sent anywhere.

You grant this permission through iOS, and you can withdraw it
at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security >
Media & Apple Music.

**Your alarm and playback preferences.** Your wake time, days,
volume and fade settings, snooze duration, and playlist or
genre selections are saved on your device so the app remembers
them between launches.

## What Soundrise does not do

- It does not collect analytics or usage data.
- It does not use crash reporting.
- It does not contain third-party advertising or tracking
  software.
- It does not have a server, an account system, or a login.
- It does not send your data anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

We have no way to see your music library, your settings, or how
you use the app.

## Where your data is stored

All of it is stored locally on your device, using standard iOS
storage. Nothing is uploaded.

If you have iCloud device backups enabled, your app settings
may be included in your own encrypted iCloud backup. That
backup is managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and
we have no access to it.

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything Soundrise has stored by using
**Delete My Data** in the app's Settings, or by deleting the
app from your device. Either removes your saved preferences
and cancels any scheduled alarms.

Withdrawing Soundrise's access to your Apple Music library is
done separately, in the iOS Settings app under Privacy &
Security > Media & Apple Music. The app's Settings screen has
a **Manage Apple Music Access** shortcut that takes you there.

## Apple Music

Soundrise plays music through Apple Music using Apple's
MusicKit framework. Your use of Apple Music is governed by
Apple's own privacy policy and terms, which are separate from
this one. Soundrise does not receive information about your
Apple ID or your Apple Music account.

## Children

Soundrise does not collect personal information from anyone,
including children.
